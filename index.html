About me

Curious mind, steady hands.

I'm Karen Albertini, and I'm studying Mechanical Engineering at the DHBW. The dual study program means I switch between lectures at university and hands-on work at my partner company, Otto Egelhof, so I get to learn the theory and see it in action.

I'm half French, half Japanese, and I speak four languages: French, Japanese, German and English.

My path here started with my hands: making jewellery, sewing, always building something. Engineering felt like the natural next step.

What I love most about engineering is that moment when something on a screen becomes real. I enjoy design, problem solving and figuring out how things work — and I'm always happy to take something apart (and put it back together).

Outside of engineering you'll often find me at my sewing machine — making clothes from scratch or upcycling old ones into something new.
